FAQ

Common Questions About Safety Nets

How long does installation take?
Most balcony installations take 1–2 hours. Full building or industrial jobs may take a day. Our team completes a free site inspection first and gives you an exact timeline before we start.
What material are your nets made of?
High-tenacity HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) nylon that is UV-stabilised, weatherproof and rot-proof — engineered specifically for Chennai sun and monsoon exposure.
Will the net be visible from inside my home?
Our nets are nearly transparent from a few feet away. Combined with matching stainless-steel wire framing, they blend into the balcony railing and do not obstruct your view.
Do you offer a warranty?
Yes — we offer a long-term warranty on both the netting material and our workmanship. Full terms are shared during the free site inspection.
Which areas of Chennai do you cover?
All of Chennai — Anna Nagar, Adyar, Vadapalani, Mylapore, T. Nagar, Guindy, Ashok Nagar, K.K. Nagar, Kodambakkam, Chromepet, Egmore, Madhavaram, Poonamallee and more.
