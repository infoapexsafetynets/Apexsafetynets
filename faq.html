Safety Nets in Chennai — FAQ

Everything Chennai homeowners, apartment associations and businesses ask us — answered honestly.

How much do safety nets cost in Chennai?
Pricing depends on balcony area, net type and material. We provide a free site inspection with an itemised quote — no obligation. As a direct manufacturer with wholesale rates, our pricing is consistently the lowest in Chennai for the same net quality.
How long does installation take?
Most balcony installations are completed in 1–2 hours. Full-building anti-bird netting or industrial fall-arrest nets may take a day. We finish 95% of residential jobs on the same visit as inspection.
What material are Apex nets made of?
We use high-tenacity HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) nylon, UV-stabilised for Chennai sun and treated to resist rot, mildew and rain. Framing is done with marine-grade stainless-steel wire and anchors.
Are the nets visible from inside my apartment?
No — our nets are nearly transparent from more than a few feet away. When viewed against sky or foliage, most customers forget the net is there at all. The stainless framing blends into your existing balcony rail.
Will installation damage my walls?
We use minimal drilling with small (6–8 mm) marine-grade anchors placed at optimal points. No cutting, no cracking. When you eventually remove the net, only tiny sealable holes remain.
Do you offer a warranty?
Yes — we offer a long-term warranty on both the netting material and installation workmanship. If the net frays prematurely or any anchor loosens under normal conditions, we replace or refit it free of charge.
Are the nets safe for children and pets?
Yes. Our children safety nets are specifically engineered with tighter mesh spacing and higher tensile strength for upper-floor balconies. The mesh is small enough to prevent falls but open enough for full airflow and light.
Do you handle apartment associations / entire buildings?
Yes — we regularly service entire apartment complexes, IT parks, schools, hospitals and public buildings. Association secretaries can call us for bulk-installation quotes.
Which areas of Chennai do you cover?
All of Chennai — Anna Nagar, Adyar, Vadapalani, Mylapore, T. Nagar, Guindy, Ashok Nagar, K.K. Nagar, Kodambakkam, Chromepet, Egmore, Madhavaram, Poonamallee, Meenambakkam and every other neighbourhood.
Are you available on Sundays and public holidays?
Yes — Apex is open 24/7, all seven days including public holidays. Emergency installations (e.g. after a fall risk is spotted) can typically be handled within a few hours.
How do I book an inspection?
Call 094935 47222, WhatsApp us, or fill the booking form on any page. Our team confirms within 30 minutes and schedules a free site visit at your convenience.
What happens during the free site inspection?
Our team measures your balcony/window, discusses the right net type for your needs, shows you material samples, and provides an on-the-spot itemised quote. There is zero obligation to proceed.
