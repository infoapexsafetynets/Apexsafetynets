Our Story

Started on one balcony in Vadapalani. Now covering all of Chennai.

Apex Safety Nets began in August 2005 with a single order — a pigeon net for a Vadapalani balcony. Word spread. Twenty years later we are Chennai largest independent safety-nets installer, with over 1000 installations in homes, offices, IT parks and apartment complexes across every neighbourhood.

We have always done one thing and done it well: invisible, durable, honestly priced netting. No franchisees, no sub-contractors — every installer wears an Apex uniform and reports to the same team you spoke to on the phone.

Today we serve residential apartments, IT companies, schools, hospitals, construction sites and public parks across Chennai. And we still turn up in 30 minutes for a free site inspection.
