FAQ

Common questions about balcony bird nets

Whats the difference between balcony pigeon nets and balcony bird nets?
Pigeon nets have 50 mm mesh (only stops birds pigeon-sized and larger). Balcony bird nets use 19–30 mm mesh to also block crows, mynas, sparrows and small birds.
Do birds die in the net?
No. The net is a barrier — birds see it and avoid it, or land elsewhere. No entanglement.
Which mesh size should I pick?
If you only see pigeons — 50 mm. If you see sparrows and crows too — 30 mm. If you have severe sparrow/bat issues — 19 mm.
